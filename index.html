About Banshan

A nimble partner
for moving markets.

Banshan Fashion Jewelry Co., Ltd. is a Yiwu-based trading and manufacturing partner, building wearable collections for fast-moving fashion markets.

With around 50 people and a dedicated sampling team of around ten, we support development from reference or sketch through production and packing.

Our strength is practical: reliable quality, balanced pricing and samples that arrive quickly enough to keep your calendar moving.
